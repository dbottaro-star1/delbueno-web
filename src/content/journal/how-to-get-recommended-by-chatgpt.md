You cannot buy or force a recommendation from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity, but you can make your startup easy for them to find, understand and trust. Answer the questions your buyers ask in plain, quotable sentences, describe the company the same way everywhere, and earn mentions on third-party sites the assistants already read.

Structured data and llms.txt help machines parse your site, but they support that work rather than replace it. Anyone who guarantees a placement in AI answers is promising something they do not control.

## What do AI assistants look at?

When an assistant searches the web to answer a question, it tends to quote pages that answer that exact question, and it weighs what other sites say about you next to what you say about yourself. Several assistants search through Bing or Google, so a site that is not indexed in both starts behind.

## What should you change on your own website?

- **One sentence that says what you are.** What you do, for whom and where, on the home and about pages, repeated word for word in every profile.
- **Pages titled with real questions.** Write the question the way a buyer would ask it, then answer it in the first two sentences. This page follows that format.
- **Structured data.** Organization data with links to your profiles, plus FAQPage markup on pages that carry a frequently asked questions section.
- **llms.txt.** A [proposed standard](https://llmstxt.org/) for summarizing your site for language models. Support is uneven, so treat it as cheap insurance.
- **Crawlable, fast pages.** In robots.txt, allow the AI crawlers you want reading your site.

## What happens off your website?

This part often matters more. Complete your profiles on the review directories your buyers use, with the same description as your site, and ask real clients for reviews. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does llms.txt get my startup recommended by ChatGPT?

Not on its own. It helps a model read your site, but AI companies do not all use it, and a recommendation depends far more on clear answers and what other sites say about you.

### How long does it take to show up in AI answers?

There is no fixed timeline. New pages need to be crawled and indexed, and third-party mentions build slowly. Measure over months, not weeks.

### How do you measure AI visibility?

Ask the same set of buyer questions in several assistants every month and note who gets named, and track referral traffic from AI domains in your analytics. Answers change from one run to the next, so look at the trend, not a single answer.
