## Overview

1to1000 is an AI-powered real estate platform that replaces the "search and scroll" fatigue of traditional property apps with a high-intent matching engine. Think of it as the "Tinder for Real Estate": compatibility scores connect properties with the right people, for landlords, renters, buyers and realtors.

Our job at delbueno Studio was to turn this multi-persona vision into one cohesive MVP for both iOS and Android. We balanced a minimalist, swipe-to-match experience for property seekers with high-density dashboards for real estate professionals.

## The challenge

The main hurdle was user fragmentation. We were not building one app but four distinct experiences under one roof.

- **The conflict.** How do you keep a simple swipe-to-match interface for renters while giving realtors and landlords the data-dense dashboards they need?
- **The technical constraint.** The app needed a unified feel across iOS and Android while respecting each platform's native behavior, from Human Interface Guidelines to Material Design.
- **Founder collaboration.** Feature creep was a risk. Working with the founders, we had to prioritize which AI features would deliver the most value at launch, and make the case for that scope with real value and proof.

## What we did

- User research and persona validation for all four user types
- Product design and UX/UI for a swipe-based experience for seekers and dashboards for professionals
- Interactive prototypes
- A design system that works as a foundation rather than a skin, so new property types can be added without breaking the UX
- Mobile app design that follows the best practices of each platform

## Results

The 1to1000 founders went from a concept to a market-ready product in 45 days. AI matching cut the average time a user spent searching by 40%, moving them straight to viewing. The "dating app" psychology led to 3x more daily active sessions than traditional real estate apps.
