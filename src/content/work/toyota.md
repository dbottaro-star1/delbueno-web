## Overview

This concept landing page elevates the digital presence of the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid in the Uruguayan market. We moved away from static, spec-heavy automotive pages and toward a high-end, cinematic experience that mirrors the vehicle's blend of rugged capability and hybrid efficiency.

The page is "living": it educates the visitor through movement and interactive storytelling. It combines immersive galleries, fluid 2D animations that visualize the powertrain, modular specification cards that simplify complex data, and an interactive consumption map.

## The challenge

Automotive buyers in Uruguay are increasingly conscious of efficiency, but they still expect the premium feel of an SUV. Three problems stood in the way:

- **The information load.** Car specifications are dense and unappealing. Technical hybrid data had to feel modern and easy to digest.
- **Visual storytelling.** Static images do not capture the fluid nature of a hybrid engine. We needed a way to show the transition between electric and petrol power.
- **Geographic relevance.** The abstract idea of fuel efficiency had to become tangible for a local driver.

## What we did

We focused on interaction design to make the benefits of hybrid technology tangible for the local market:

- Fluid 2D animations that visualize the powertrain switching between electric and petrol
- Modular specification cards that simplify complex data while keeping the premium look
- An interactive consumption map that lets drivers calculate real-world fuel savings on local routes
- Interactive prototypes and marketing research to shape the flow

By focusing on interaction design rather than visual design alone, we turned a traditional brochure into a persuasive sales tool. The map works as a psychological nudge, giving each visitor immediate, personalized proof of the hybrid's economic value.

## Results

The page turns car buying from passive viewing into an active cost-benefit analysis. We project engagement 64%+ higher through interactivity, with a streamlined path to conversion and an experience that holds up across devices. By quantifying hybrid efficiency through the map, the page gives buyers a logical justification for the premium price and shortens the consideration phase.
